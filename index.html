WHY CHOOSE US

PulseTech Solutions is a website development, graphic design, and digital marketing studio in Kampala. We also install business systems for a shop, a restaurant, a school, or a clinic.

A website is built for a phone and a desk, prepared so search engines can read it, and handed to your staff so they can edit the pages. A logo, card, flyer, or stationery set comes as a print-ready file that still holds on a sign, a shirt, and the website. Social media, search, and ads are planned around the enquiries you want, and checked against those enquiries. A system is installed on site, filled with the records you already keep, and the people who will use it are trained before we leave.
