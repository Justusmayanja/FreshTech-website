Terms of service

The terms for work you order from PulseTech Solutions. Updated 6 October 2026.

Who these terms cover

These terms apply when you ask PulseTech Solutions, Kampala, Uganda, to build a website, prepare design files, run social media or other marketing, or install a business system. Placing an order, paying a deposit, or asking us to start is acceptance of these terms.

Quotes and payment

Prices on this site are in US dollars. You can pay by MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, bank transfer, or the method written on the invoice. If you pay in shillings, the shilling figure is fixed on the invoice using the rate that day.

A quote is good for 14 days. Websites, design, and marketing start after a 50% deposit. The balance is due before launch, or before print files are released. Days listed on a package are working days, and they start once we have the deposit and the text, photos, or records we asked for.

What we need from you

You send the name, wording, photos, and records the job needs, and you confirm they are yours to use. If those arrive late, the delivery date moves by the same number of days. We will not invent claims about your business.

Websites

A website package includes the pages and features listed on that package. Hosting and the domain are arranged with you. You can edit the pages after handover. Extra pages, a shop, or Mobile Money that were not in the package are quoted before we add them.

Design

Logos, cards, flyers, and stationery are delivered as print-ready files. Printing is a separate arrangement if you want us to handle it. We release the source files when the package includes them and the balance is paid.

Social media and marketing

A monthly package is the management listed on that package, such as posts, captions, or ad setup. It is not the money you pay Google or Meta for ads. That ad spend is yours. A month starts when the deposit is in and we have the offer you want published. You can stop a monthly plan with notice before the next month is billed.

Business systems

A till, school system, clinic record, or similar system is installed for your organisation. It is not a file you download. Setup, import of the records you already keep, and staff training are included. Most systems are ready in two to four weeks after the deposit and those records. Mobile Money, when ordered, is added to a site or till you already have and is tested before it is treated as live.

Your name and our portfolio

You keep the rights to your own name, logo, photos, and content. We keep the right to show the finished site or design in our portfolio unless you ask us in writing not to.

If the work stops

If you cancel after we have started, the deposit covers the work already done and is not returned. If we cannot finish for a reason on our side, we refund the part of the fee that has no work against it.
