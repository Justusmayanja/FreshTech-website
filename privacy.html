Privacy policy

How PulseTech Solutions handles the details you send us. Updated 6 October 2026.

Who we are

PulseTech Solutions is a design and technology studio in Kampala, Uganda. This policy covers the website you are on, the messages you send us, and the work that follows from those messages.

Write to pulsetechsolutions.info@gmail.com or WhatsApp +256752895268.

What you send us

When you enquire, we receive the details you choose to give: your name, email, phone number, and the message itself. A website, design, or system job may also include logos, photos, student or product lists, and other records you ask us to use.

The contact form on this site opens your own email program and addresses the message to us. It does not save a copy on a server of ours. WhatsApp messages stay in that chat.

The cart

Items you add to the cart are stored in your browser on this device. They are not sent to us until you choose checkout, which opens WhatsApp with the list. Clearing the cart, or clearing your browser storage, removes them.

Newsletter box

The newsletter form on this site shows a thank-you note in the page. It does not transmit your name or email to a server. If you later write to us and ask to be on the list, we keep that email only to send notes from this office, and we remove it when you ask.

Invoices and payment

When you pay, we keep the invoice and the payment reference from MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, or the bank, the same way any Kampala business keeps a receipt. We do not store card numbers. We do not sell lists, and we do not pass your number to other studios.

How long we keep it

Enquiry messages are kept while the job is open and for the invoice record after it. Records you gave us for a system, such as a product list or a student register, are used for that install and are not reused on another client’s work.

Asking us to delete something

Write to the email above and say what you want removed. We will delete enquiry copies we still hold, except where an invoice has to stay for the books.

Need a website, a system, or help on social media?

Tell us what you need. We reply from this office in Kampala.
