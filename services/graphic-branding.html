Our branding process

From research to rollout in 5 strategic phases.

1. Brand Discovery

Questionnaire, competitor audit, moodboard, and positioning workshop.

2. Concept Development

3-5 logo directions with rationale. We explore typography, iconography, color.

3. Refinement

Chosen concept refined with 2 revision rounds. Lockups and variations finalized.

4. Brand Guidelines

Comprehensive style guide: logo usage, colors, fonts, spacing, do's/don'ts.

5. Asset Delivery

All file formats (AI, EPS, SVG, PNG), templates, and brand kit packaged.
