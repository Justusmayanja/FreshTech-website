Frequently asked questions

Common questions about our social media growth service.

Do you post on our accounts or create new ones?

We post on your existing accounts with your permission. If you don't have accounts yet, we help you set them up with proper branding and bios.

Do you come to our location to film?

Yes! We travel to your school, office, shop, or event venue in Kampala and surrounding areas. On-site filming is included in our Growth and Premium packages.

What does "routine daily posting on X" mean?

We post on your X (Twitter) account every day — announcements, updates, threads, and engagement content — so your brand stays visible and active without gaps.

Can you coach our staff who are shy on camera?

Absolutely. Staff coaching is a core part of our service. We guide your team on what to say, how to stand, and how to present confidently — most people improve dramatically after one session.

How long before we see results?

Most clients see improved engagement within the first month. Significant follower growth typically builds over 2–3 months of consistent, quality posting.

Can we upgrade or downgrade our package?

Yes. Packages are flexible — you can upgrade as you grow or adjust based on your needs. We recommend starting with Growth for the best balance of platforms and video content.
