Our UI/UX process

User-centered design methodology in 5 phases.

1. Research & Discovery

User interviews, analytics review, competitive analysis, persona development.

2. Information Architecture

Sitemap, user flows, task analysis, and content strategy.

3. Wireframing

Low-fidelity wireframes, interaction patterns, layout exploration.

4. Visual Design

High-fidelity mockups in Figma with design system foundations.

5. Prototype & Test

Interactive prototype, usability testing, iteration based on feedback.
