Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services

What services do you offer?

We offer website development, custom software & POS systems, Mobile Money integration, social media growth, digital marketing (SEO, social media, ads), and IT consultation. Each service is customised to meet your organisation's needs across Uganda.

Can you integrate Mobile Money and other payment systems?

Absolutely! We specialise in MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money integration for websites, e-commerce stores, and custom software — with secure payment flows, proper error handling, and testing before go-live.

Do you provide software setup and training?

Yes! We implement school management systems, POS platforms, and custom business software tailored to your workflow. Our service includes setup, data migration where needed, and hands-on team training so your staff can use the system confidently.

What is included in your digital marketing service?

Our digital marketing service includes SEO optimisation for the Ugandan market, social media management (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X), content creation, paid advertising campaigns, performance tracking, and monthly reports to help you reach more customers.

Do you offer maintenance and support after project completion?

Yes, we provide ongoing maintenance and support. All packages include initial support (30–90 days depending on the package). After that, we offer flexible maintenance plans covering updates, security, backups, and technical support. Our Premium package includes 3 months of free maintenance.
