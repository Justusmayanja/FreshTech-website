Our Story

Founded with a vision to serve Kenyan businesses, KenZobe Technologies has been at the forefront of digital transformation for over 5 years. Our journey began in the heart of Nairobi's tech hub, Eastleigh, where we set out to make technology accessible and affordable for businesses of all sizes.

What started as a small team of passionate developers has grown into a full-service technology company, serving clients across Kenya and beyond. We've had the privilege of working with startups, SMEs, and established enterprises, helping them leverage technology to achieve their business goals.

Our Vision

To be Kenya's most trusted technology partner, empowering businesses to thrive in the digital age through innovative, accessible, and reliable solutions.

Our Mission

Making technology accessible and affordable for all businesses. We believe every business deserves high-quality digital solutions that help them grow, compete, and succeed in today's digital landscape.

Based at BBS Mall in Eastleigh, we're proud to be part of Nairobi's vibrant tech ecosystem. Our location reflects our commitment to being accessible to our clients while being at the center of innovation and entrepreneurship in Kenya.

What We Do

We offer comprehensive technology solutions designed to help your business succeed in the digital world. From custom web and mobile applications to AI-powered automation and digital marketing, we provide end-to-end services that drive growth and innovation.
