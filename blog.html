How to Accept Mobile Money on Your Website

In Uganda, most customers pay with MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money — not cards. If you sell products or services online, integrating mobile payments directly on your website removes friction and increases completed sales.

Why integrate on-site instead of manual payments?

Manual “send to this number” workflows lose customers who forget reference codes or pay wrong amounts. On-site integration confirms payment automatically, updates order status, and gives customers confidence that your business is professional.

Common integration options

  • MTN Mobile Money API — direct integration for merchants with MTN business accounts
  • Airtel Money API — similar flow for Airtel merchant setups
  • Payment aggregators — third-party gateways that bundle multiple methods
  • Custom checkout flows — for software, school fees, or booking systems

The implementation process

PulseTech handles merchant application guidance, sandbox testing, secure callback URLs, and production deployment. We test with small real transactions before go-live. Typical integration projects start from $55 depending on complexity.

Security and compliance

Never store mobile money PINs on your server. Use official APIs, HTTPS everywhere, and server-side payment verification. We build reconciliation reports so your accounts team can match payments to orders daily.

How to Choose a Web Developer

Not all web developers in Kampala deliver the same quality. Some disappear after launch; others use templates and charge premium prices. Here is how to choose a partner you can trust.

Check their live portfolio

Ask for Ugandan sites they have built — not mockups. Open them on your phone. Are they fast? Do forms work? Is content current? PulseTech maintains live client sites across NGOs, schools, finance, and real estate.

Ask for the price in dollars

Avoid a quote that says “we will see.” You should get a written scope with a total in US dollars. Our basic website package starts at $120, which is about UGX 450,000 at a rate near 3,700.

Ask about post-launch support

Websites need updates, security patches, and occasional fixes. Confirm support duration, response times, and cost of changes after launch. PulseTech includes 30–90 days support on website packages.

Questions to ask before signing

  • Will I own the domain and hosting accounts?
  • Can I update content myself — will you train me?
  • Do you understand Mobile Money and local payment methods?
  • What is the timeline from deposit to launch?
  • Who hosts the site and what are annual renewal costs?

SEO: How to Rank on Google

When someone searches “web developer Kampala” or “school website Uganda,” you want your business to appear. Search engine optimisation (SEO) makes that happen — without paying for every click.

Start with Google Business Profile

Claim your listing, add accurate address (Kampala, Uganda, etc.), phone number, hours, and photos. Encourage satisfied clients to leave reviews. This helps you appear on Google Maps — critical for local service businesses.

On-page SEO basics

  • Use clear page titles with location keywords (e.g. “Website Design Kampala”)
  • Write unique meta descriptions for each page
  • Structure content with proper headings (H1, H2, H3)
  • Optimise images with descriptive alt text
  • Ensure fast load times on mobile networks

Content that works in Uganda

Publish helpful articles answering questions your customers ask — pricing, processes, local regulations. Blog posts like this one attract organic traffic over time. Write in plain English; many searchers use mobile data and skim quickly.

Technical SEO

Your site needs HTTPS, mobile-responsive layout, clean URLs, and a sitemap submitted to Google Search Console. Every PulseTech website package includes SEO-ready structure; Business and Premium tiers add advanced optimisation.

POS Systems vs Spreadsheets

Many shops in Kampala start with notebooks and Excel. That works until stock discrepancies, slow checkout queues, and missing sales reports become daily problems. A point-of-sale (POS) system solves this.

When you have outgrown spreadsheets

  • You cannot track stock accurately across shifts
  • End-of-day reconciliation takes hours
  • Staff disputes over cash drawer totals
  • You need sales reports by product, day, or cashier
  • Customers expect receipts and faster checkout

Features to look for in a Ugandan POS

Choose a system that works offline when internet drops, supports multiple users with permissions, prints receipts, tracks inventory in real time, and optionally integrates Mobile Money for customer payments. PulseTech builds retail and restaurant POS from $270 depending on features.

Implementation timeline

Most POS deployments take 2–4 weeks including setup, product import, staff training, and trial period. We stay available for adjustments after launch.

Why Schools Need a Management System

Schools across Uganda still juggle paper registers, WhatsApp groups, and manual fee ledgers. A combined website and school management system saves time, reduces errors, and improves communication with parents.

Problems paper-based admin creates

Growing on TikTok & Instagram

Younger audiences in Kampala and across Uganda discover brands on TikTok and Instagram before they ever visit a website. Consistent, authentic content builds trust faster than occasional posts.

Content that performs locally

Show real premises, real staff, and real customer outcomes — not stock footage. Short vertical videos (15–60 seconds) work best. Schools might show classroom moments; retailers show products; NGOs show impact.

Posting schedule

Aim for 3–5 posts per week on primary platforms. TikTok favours frequency; Instagram rewards Reels. PulseTech’s social media packages include on-site filming, editing, and daily posting on X for brands that need full coverage.

When to hire a team instead of DIY

If leadership has no time to film, staff lack confidence on camera, or growth has stalled for months — a dedicated team helps. Social management starts at $80 a month. Filming and extra platforms are scoped on top of that.

E-Commerce Website Development

Online selling in Uganda is growing — but success requires more than a product catalogue. You need Mobile Money checkout, reliable hosting, clear delivery policies, and inventory control.

Essential e-commerce features

  • Product catalogue with categories and search
  • Shopping cart and order confirmation
  • MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money payment
  • Order management dashboard for your team
  • Mobile-first design (most buyers use phones)

Logistics and trust

State delivery areas, timelines, and costs clearly. Display phone and WhatsApp for support. Show real product photos — customers in Uganda are cautious about online fraud. SSL (padlock icon) is mandatory.

Budget in dollars

A smaller shop can start on the ecommerce package at $220, or $420 if you need about 50 products, payments, and on-page search work. Larger catalogues are quoted separately. We help with the domain and hosting.
