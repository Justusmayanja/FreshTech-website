About FreshTech

Building digital futures since 2020

We're a hybrid digital agency and tech accessories shop. We design websites, craft brand identities, and sell premium tech gear—all under one roof.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, FreshTech Solutions started as a boutique web design agency. As we grew, we noticed our clients constantly asking where to source quality tech accessories that matched their new brand aesthetics.

So we built a curated tech shop alongside our agency services. Today, we're a one-stop destination for businesses that want stunning digital presence and premium accessories to match.

We've served 200+ clients across startups, scale-ups, and enterprises—from SaaS platforms to DTC e-commerce brands.
