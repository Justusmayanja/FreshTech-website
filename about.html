About Us

PulseTech Solutions is a Kampala studio of website developers, graphic designers, and digital marketers. We also install the business systems our clients use every day.

Our Story

We are website developers, graphic designers, and digital marketers. From this office in Kampala we build company sites and online shops, prepare logos and print, and run the social media, search, and ads that follow. When the job is a till or a set of records, we install that too.

One team takes the brief and finishes the work. Before we start, the job is written down: what the site, the design, or the system must do, and what your staff will be able to run when it is handed over.
